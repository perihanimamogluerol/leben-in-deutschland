Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

The app requires no user account and contains no advertising, analytics SDKs, or crash tracking.

1. Data controller

The party responsible for the “Leben in Deutschland” app is:

Next Day Mobile Studio
Frankfurt am Main, Germany
Email: nextdaymobilestudio@gmail.com

2. Data processed by the app

Next Day Mobile Studio does not collect names, email addresses, telephone numbers, location data, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or payment information through the app. Registration is not required.

The following information is stored only on your device so the learning features can work:

  • selected language and German federal state,
  • learning progress and answered questions,
  • incorrectly answered questions,
  • mock-exam results and history, and
  • other local app preferences.

Next Day Mobile Studio does not receive this locally stored information and cannot associate it with an individual.

3. Internet connection and content delivery

The app uses an internet connection to provide or update the question catalogue, language packs, explanations, memory aids, and images associated with questions.

  • Google Firebase Cloud Firestore: The app downloads content files from Firestore. Google may process technically necessary connection information, such as the IP address and information about the app, platform, and version (the “Firebase User Agent”).
  • GitHub Pages: Certain question images may be downloaded from GitHub Pages. Standard technical connection information, particularly the IP address and user agent, may therefore be transmitted to GitHub.

Next Day Mobile Studio does not use these services for personalised advertising, user profiling, or analytics.

4. Android backup

Depending on your Android and Google Account settings, the operating system may back up local app data as part of device backup. This process is controlled by Android or Google. Next Day Mobile Studio cannot access the backup. Backups can be managed in your device or Google Account settings.

5. Website and Netlify

This website is hosted by Netlify. When the website is accessed, Netlify may process technically necessary access information in server logs, such as the IP address, time, requested page, browser type, and device information. Next Day Mobile Studio does not place analytics or advertising cookies on this website.

6. Sharing and sale of data

Next Day Mobile Studio does not sell personal data or share data for advertising or marketing purposes. Technical data may be processed only by the service providers described above where necessary to deliver app content and this website, or where required by law.

7. Retention and deletion

Local learning and preference data remains on your device until you clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the app. The usual path is:

Android Settings → Apps → Leben in Deutschland → Storage → Clear data

Retention of technical logs by Google, GitHub, and Netlify is governed by their respective policies. Support emails are retained only for as long as necessary to resolve the request and meet any applicable legal record-keeping obligations.

8. Security

Data transmitted between the app, website, and the services used is generally encrypted using HTTPS. Despite appropriate technical measures, no data transmission can be guaranteed to be entirely risk-free.

9. Children

The app is designed to help people prepare for the “Leben in Deutschland” test or the German naturalisation test and is not specifically directed at children. Next Day Mobile Studio does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Your rights

Where personal data is processed, you may have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection. Because learning progress and preferences are stored only on your device, Next Day Mobile Studio cannot view or centrally delete that information. Contact us at any time with privacy questions.
